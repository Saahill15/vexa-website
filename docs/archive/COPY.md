## Current proposition

VEXA develops gaming campaigns around a brand's business problem. For established esports organisations, it develops the content, audience evidence and commercial offer behind a partnership. Kaustubh teaches players, creators and team owners how the business works.

Home leads with the brand offer, shows a labelled illustrative example and gives each audience a clear next step. The organisation page explains the work and the team's responsibility to deliver. The free guides carry a worked example and a usable worksheet. Contact submits a brief for email follow-up; it does not book a call or promise a research report.

Valuate is presented as deal preparation with an indicative price check inside it. A calculated estimate is not a verified fair market price. Its local working link is only visible in preview mode.
