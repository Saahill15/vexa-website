Three ways a brand enters gaming

Most briefs collapse “gaming” into one buy. It is not one buy. You are choosing an association. We help you pick which layer, and why that layer, before anyone spends national money.

Tournament

A league, event, or series your category can stand next to. Useful when you need a moment, a title, and a defined audience window. Wrong when the event is loud and the buyer fit is thin.

Club

A team or org with a roster, a fanbase, and match-day attention. Useful when you want repeated presence. Wrong when you are buying a logo and hoping the fans care.

Creator

A voice that already talks to the players you want. Useful when trust and craft matter more than a stadium. Wrong when the follower count is the whole brief.

How a brand enters gaming with us

Process, not trophies. You will not find a wall of invented wins here. You will find the order of work.

STEP 1

Discovery call

Free. Kaustubh runs it. You talk about the brand, your role, why gaming is on the table, and what you have already tried. We do not pitch on this call.

STEP 2

Diagnosis and research

We look at the category, the titles that matter, and the tournament, club, or creator associations that could actually move your buyer.

STEP 3

Thesis and pilot gates

We write the recommendation and the gates. One gate means do not scale. The other means the live test earned a full campaign.

STEP 4

Word outline

A cross-examination document your team can argue with. If the outline does not survive the room, we do not decorate it.

STEP 5

Design, then run

Bhaskar designs in Canva from that outline. You buy the plan. We run the pilot or campaign and report what happened.

A small live test before national money

Gaming looks clean in a slide and messy in market. We start with a control pilot, then a full campaign. A small live test before national spend.
